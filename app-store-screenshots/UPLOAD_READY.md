## 📱 App Store Connect Upload

### Quick Steps

1. **Go to App Store Connect**
   - URL: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
   - Sign in with Apple Developer account

2. **Navigate to Your App**
   - My Apps → Genosys Mobile App
   - Click "App Store" tab

3. **Upload Screenshots**
   - Scroll to "App Previews and Screenshots"
   - Select "6.7" Display" (iPhone 14 Pro Max and later)
   - Click "+" to add screenshots
   - Upload from: `app-store-screenshots/appstore-ready/`

4. **Set Screenshot Order**
   - Drag to reorder (first 3 shown on installation sheets)
   - Recommended order:
     1. Shop Home
     2. Product Detail
     3. Shopping Bag
     4. Checkout
     5. Profile
     6. Order Detail

5. **Save**
   - Click "Save" in top right
