New in this build: a Live Activity

The order card on the Lock Screen is a widget extension (ae.genosys.app.widgets). It needs no permission prompt beyond the system’s own Live Activities setting, which is enabled by default.

How to see it

  1. Sign in with the demo account above.
  2. Add any product to the bag and check out choosing Cash on Delivery — no payment details are required for this path.
  3. Lock the device. The card appears on the Lock Screen with the order number, its current status, the delivery window for the chosen emirate, and a three-step tracker.

The card is also raised and updated by push while the app is closed, over APNs on the ae.genosys.app.push-type.liveactivity topic. Requires iOS 16.2 or later; on earlier versions the app behaves exactly as 1.11.0 did.

Permissions and data

Unchanged from 1.11.0. Push notifications remain optional and the app is fully functional without them. No new data is collected for this release.
