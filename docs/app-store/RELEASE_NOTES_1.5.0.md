## App Store "What's New" Text

> Copy the text below into App Store Connect → "What's New in This Version"

NEW: Skin Concern Pages
Browse 8 curated skincare categories — Sun Protection, Acne, Pigmentation, Scars, Hair Loss, Anti-Aging, Hydration, and Sensitivity. Each page features expert routines, product recommendations, professional protocol PDFs, and FAQs.

Tap-to-Add from Routines
Tap any product in a skincare routine to instantly add it to your bag. Tap again to remove. Long press to view product details. Haptic feedback and animated confirmations included.

Enjoy tactile feedback across all interactions — cart actions, navigation, favorites, and more.
