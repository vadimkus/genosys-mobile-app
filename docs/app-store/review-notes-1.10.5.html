App Overview

GENOSYS UAE is the official e-commerce app of GENOSYS Middle East, the exclusive UAE distributor of GENOSYS professional Korean dermacosmetics. Customers browse and purchase authentic, Dubai Municipality–certified skincare, with AI skin analysis, an in-app assistant, product guides, order tracking, and full English / Arabic / Russian support (Arabic is right-to-left).

What's New in This Version

  • Smarter search — finds products regardless of word order, now including Arabic and Russian search.

  • Faster, cleaner product pages with sharper images.
  • Account security — improved sign-in and session protection; in-app account deletion available (Profile → Edit → Delete account).
  • Bug fixes and performance improvements.
